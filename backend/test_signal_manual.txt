SIGNAL FAILURE PROCEDURE - STATION OPERATIONS MANUAL

Section 4.2: Signal Failure Response

When a signal failure is detected at any station, staff must follow this procedure:

1. Immediately notify the central control room.
2. Dispatch a technician to the affected signal within 15 minutes.
3. If the failure occurs during peak hours, implement manual signaling with a qualified signalman to maintain reduced train flow.
4. Trains should be slowed to 15 km/h when passing through the affected signal zone.
5. If resolution exceeds 45 minutes, consider single-line working on the adjacent track.
6. Document the incident including duration, number of affected trains, and resolution steps taken.

Signal failures during peak hours typically require additional staffing and can cause delays propagating to 2-3 subsequent stations if not managed within 60 minutes.
